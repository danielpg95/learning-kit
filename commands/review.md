description: Review recent changes through a learning lens
argument-hint: [optional: file or feature to review]
---

Activate the review-my-code skill.

If $ARGUMENTS specifies a file or feature, review that specifically.
Otherwise, review the most recently changed files in this session.

Focus on:
- Patterns the user might not fully understand yet
- Mistakes that reveal a gap in understanding (not just typos)
- Opportunities to introduce a better pattern

Do NOT just list issues. For each one, teach why it matters and what
the better approach looks like.
